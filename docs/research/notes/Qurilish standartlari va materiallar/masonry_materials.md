# Masonry materials (brick, blocks, mortar, lintels, mesh): standards, consumption and labour norms (RU / EU-DE / UZ)

Scope note: numbers are quoted from primary standard texts (meganorm.ru mirrors of GOST, SP, GESN; SNiP PDF) where they could be retrieved. Values I derived by geometry are marked **[calc]**. Values from manufacturers, calculators or popular sites are marked **[mfr]** / **[secondary]**. "App use:" says how the mobile estimating + drawing app could use each fact.

## 1. Brick formats and grades: GOST 530-2012 (ceramic), GOST 379-2015 (silicate), DIN/EN formats, and what is used in Uzbekistan

### Takeaway
Russia/CIS ceramic brick is built on the 250×120 mm footprint, with heights 65 (1NF), 88 (1.4NF) and 140 mm for the 2.1NF stone (GOST 530-2012 says 140, not 138; 138 is the **silicate** stone under GOST 379-2015). Large porous blocks go up to 14.3NF (510×250×219). Ceramic **brick** strength grades are M100–M300. M75 exists only for stones and horizontal-void products, so the brief's "M75–M300" range is slightly wrong for brick. German formats sit on a 240×115 mm footprint (DF/NF/2DF/3DF). Uzbekistan's market uses 250×120×65 and 250×120×88 (plus some 230×115×65). Uzbekistan did **not** vote for GOST 530-2012, and I found no Uzbek O'z DSt brick standard.

### Cited Findings

#### Uzbekistan

App use: per-standard `adopted_in` flags.

### Inferences
- The brief's "2.1NF = 250×120×138" mixes two standards. Ceramic 2.1NF stone = 250×120×140 (GOST 530-2012 Table 3); silicate stone = 250×120×138 (GOST 379-2015). The app should store exact H per product, not per "NF" code.
- "NF factor" = volume / (250×120×65). E.g., 10.7NF ≈ 380×250×219 / 1,950,000 mm³ ≈ 10.66. The app can compute NF-equivalents to normalize price per 1NF or per m³ **[calc]**.
- For UZ users, defaulting to Russian GOST nomenclature (1NF/1.4NF, M-grades, F-grades) matches market listings. Mark the legal status as "interstate GOST, adoption in UZ unverified".

### Gaps
- No Uzbek national brick standard (O'z DSt) was found, and the GOST 530-2012 adoption status in Uzbekistan is unknown. Uzstandart (agency.uz) and lex.uz were not reachable/searched successfully.
- EN 771-1 / EN 771-2 / EN 771-4 / EN 772 content (tolerance categories T1/T2/Tm, range R1/R2, Category I/II units, EN 772-1 compressive test, EN 772-3/-16 dimensions) was **not retrieved from a source**. From background knowledge only (verify): EN 771 declares values rather than fixing formats, and Germany fixes formats/classes via DIN 20000-401/-402 and DIN 105-100.
- DIN 105 (current part numbering, e.g., DIN 105-100) was not verified.
- Brick unit weights and pallet quantities (pcs/pallet) were not sourced. They are manufacturer-specific and should go in supplier catalog fields.

## 2. Aerated (gas) concrete blocks, concrete/claydite stones: GOST 31360-2024 / 31359, GOST 6133-2019, EN 771-4

### Takeaway
GOST 31360-2007 is **superseded** in Russia by **GOST 31360-2024** (in force 01.01.2025). It defines accuracy Category I (±3/±2/±1 mm, intended for thin-joint glue) and Category II (for ordinary mortar), minimum class B1.5, and F25 for external walls. Glue consumption is ~15–20 kg per m³ of masonry at a 2 mm joint (manufacturer data). Concrete wall stones (cinder/claydite "шлакоблок/керамзитоблок") follow GOST 6133-2019, typical 390×190×188, and Uzbekistan voted for it.

### Cited Findings
- **GOST 31360-2024** «Изделия стеновые неармированные из ячеистого бетона автоклавного твердения»: approved by Rosstandart order 533-ст of 24.04.2024, **effective 01.01.2025, replaces GOST 31360-2007**. Companion GOST 31359-2024 is shown as "not yet in force" on the mirror. — [meganorm GOST 31360-2024](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm/gost_gosudarstvennyj-standart/0/gost_31360-2024_mezhgosudarstvennyy_standart_izdeliya_dlya.html). App use: `standard_ref` with `valid_from`/`superseded_by` fields.
- Max unit sizes (cl. 4.2.2): length 1500, width 650, height 1000 mm (other sizes by order, cl. 4.2.4). — same source. App use: input validation bounds for custom block sizes.
- **Accuracy categories (Table 1, cl. 4.2.3):** Cat. I: L ±3.0, W ±2.0, H ±1.0, diagonal diff ≤2, edge straightness ≤1, corner chips ≤5, edge chips ≤5 mm. Cat. II: L ±4.0, W ±3.0, H ±4.0, diagonal ≤4, straightness ≤3, chips ≤10 mm. Note 4: "Изделия категории I рекомендуется применять для кладки с тонким швом." — same source. App use: catalog fields `density_grade` (D-grade), `strength_class` (B), `frost` (F), `accuracy_category`.
- Manufacturer data: 625 mm long × 250 mm high blocks in widths 75–500 mm, tolerances ±3/±2/±1 per GOST 31360. — [IstKult technical sheet](https://istkult.ru/upload/iblock/f0c/oyuia3925xfeozsbfskybt6pze7m5k63/Osnovnye_fiziko_tekhnicheskie_kharakteristiki_gazobetonnykh_blokov_Istkult.pdf) [mfr]. ### Inferences
- Blocks per m³ = 1/(L×H×W) [calc]. 600×200×300 → 27.8 pcs/m³. 625×250×300 → 21.3 pcs/m³. 600×250×200 → 33.3 pcs/m³. 600×200×100 partition → 8.33 pcs/m² of face. 625×250 face → 6.4 pcs/m². A 2 mm glue joint changes counts by <1% and can be ignored, but a 10–12 mm mortar joint for Cat. II blocks reduces the count ~2–5%. Formula with joints: N/m² = 1/((L+j_v)(H+j_h)).
- Concrete stone 390×190×188 with 10 mm vertical / 12 mm bed joints → 1/(0.40×0.20) = **12.5 stones/m²** of face [calc].

### Gaps
- GOST 31360-2024 density grade list (D300–D1200), the strength-vs-density table and the thermal conductivity table were not visible in the retrieved text. They sit in GOST 31359-2024 / 25485-2019.
- Whether Uzbekistan adopted GOST 31360-2024 (or still uses 31360-2007) is unknown.
- EN 771-4 (AAC units) and German DIN 20000-404 / Porenbeton (PP2-0.35 etc.) data were not retrieved.
- SP 339.1325800.2017 (cellular-concrete structures) glue-joint clause was not retrieved.

## 3. Wall thickness naming, bricks per m² / per m³, mortar per m³, joints, waste

### Takeaway
SP 70.13330.2012 cl. 9.2.4 fixes brick masonry joints at **12 mm horizontal / 10 mm vertical**. Acceptance tolerance is −2/+3 (bed) and ±2 (vertical), i.e., 10–15 and 8–12 mm. The popular "394 pcs/m³" assumes 10 mm joints on all faces. GESN 2020 (which assumes 12/10 joints) prices brick walls at **0.38–0.39 thousand bricks + 0.24 m³ mortar per m³** of masonry. Per-m² counts are more accurate than per-m³ for an app: 1NF ≈ 50 pcs/m² per ½-brick leaf (51 with 10 mm bed joints).

## 4. Mortar grades and mixes; cement bags per m³

### Takeaway
SP 82-101-98 gives authoritative volume proportions and cement consumption. M100 cement-sand masonry mortar is **1:4.5 by volume on PC400** (1:5.5 on PC500). For mixed cement-lime mortar, M100 needs ~**300 kg cement per m³ of mortar on PC400** (6 × 50 kg bags) or 250 kg on PC500 (5 bags). Brick masonry mortar mobility is 9–13 cm cone, and mortar must be used within 2 h. EN 998-2 classes map to the old German groups: MG II ≈ 2.5 N/mm², IIa ≈ 5, III ≈ 10, IIIa ≈ 20.

## 7. Reinforcing mesh in masonry and ties (incl. seismic)

### Takeaway
Mesh (сетчатое) reinforcement: meshes go at least every **5 courses of ordinary brick, 4 courses of thickened (1.4NF) brick, 3 courses of ceramic stones**. Wire is **Ø3–8 mm** (≤6 mm if bars cross inside the joint), with bar spacing **3–12 cm**. Joints must be ≥ bar diameter(s) + 4 mm, ≤16 mm. Seismic regions add Ø5 mm partition reinforcement every ≤700 mm, junction meshes every 700 mm (7–8 points) / 500 mm (9 points) over 1.5 m, and chain (single-row) bond.

### Cited Findings
- **SNiP II-22-81, cl. 6.76:** "Арматурные сетки следует укладывать не реже, чем через пять рядов кирпичной кладки из обыкновенного кирпича, через четыре ряда кладки из утолщенного кирпича и через три ряда кладки из керамических камней." **Cl. 6.77:** mesh diameter ≥3 mm. Max diameter in bed joints: 6 mm where bars cross in the joint, 8 mm without crossing. Bar spacing ≤12 and ≥3 cm. Joints ≥ bar diameter + 4 mm. **Cl. 6.75:** mesh only when higher brick/mortar grades and larger sections are insufficient; mesh counted in design ≥0.1% of masonry volume. **Cl. 6.43:** under heavy local loads (>80% of local bearing capacity), mesh Ø≥3 mm with cell ≤60×60 mm in ≥3 top bed joints. Pilasters: mesh every 3 courses within 1 m below the pad, embedded ≥12 cm into the wall. — [SNiP II-22-81 (1995 ed.) PDF](https://elib.spbstu.ru/dl/1000/3/5.pdf). Status: superseded by SP 15.13330.2020 (same subject; clause numbers differ). App use: mesh calculator `rows_between_meshes` default 5 (1NF) / 4 (1.4NF) / 3 (stones). Mesh area = wall length × (wall thickness − cover) per mesh layer. Validate Ø3–8 mm and joint thickness.
- **SP 70.13330.2012 cl. 9.2.14:** reinforced-masonry joints must exceed the **sum of crossing bar diameters by ≥4 mm, joint ≤16 mm**. In transverse-reinforced pillars/piers, ≥2 mesh bars must protrude 2–3 mm on the inner face (inspection). Longitudinal bars are spliced by welding; unwelded splices need hooks + wire tie with **20d** overlap. — [meganorm SP 70.13330.2012](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm_update_26042025/akt_forma/0/sp_70_13330_2012_svod_pravil_nesushchie_i_ograzhdayushchie.html). App use: when mesh is enabled, bump bed joint to ≥ (2×d + 4) mm and recompute mortar. Lap = 20d in rebar takeoff.
- SP 70 cl. 9.6.5 / 9.7.4: mesh-reinforced joints 10–16 mm (large ceramic stones) / 16 mm (silicate blocks). — same source.
- **Seismic execution, SP 70 cl. 9.11.1:** masonry laid full thickness in each course; **single-row (chain) bond**; all joints fully filled; temporary breaks only as raking (наклонная штраба) outside reinforcement zones. Cl. 9.11.6: bond strength checked at 7 days (≈50% of 28-day). Cl. 9.11.9: lintel/ring blocks must pass vertical rebar. — same source. App use: when region = seismic (UZ default), switch the bond pattern in the drawing to chain bond and the header-row rule from 1:6 to 1:1.
- **Gosarkhstroynadzor manual (seismic rows):** brick below M75 and mortar below M25 not permitted (row 26). Brick partitions reinforced with **Ø5 mm every ≤700 mm** of height (row 32). Wall junctions reinforced every **700 mm (7–8 points) / 500 mm (9 points)** over a length of 1.5 m (row 42). No masonry at negative temperature in 9-point zones; antifreeze additives required in 8-point zones (row 34). Chases: mesh ≤1.5 m vertical spacing, longitudinal bars ≤6 mm, transverse ≤3 mm, one longitudinal bar per 12 cm of wall thickness (row 13). — [dnaop Пособие госархстройнадзора](https://dnaop.com/html/31451_6.html). App use: seismic checklist + automatic mesh/tie count at T- and L-junctions = ceil(H / 0.7 or 0.5) per junction, each 1.5 m long.
- Market practice: masonry mesh Ø3–5 mm (some producers 3–6 mm, Вр-1 wire) laid every 3–5 courses. — [stroy-ka.ru blog](https://lipetsk.stroy-ka.ru/blog/kak-ispolzovat/osobennosti-ispolzovaniya-setki-kladochnoy-i-svarnoy) [secondary]; [prom.uz Вр-1 mesh listing](https://www.prom.uz/ads/setka-kladocnaya-iz-provoloki-vr-1-diametr-4-6-mm) [secondary]. App use: catalog "сетка кладочная" items (wire Ø, cell 50×50 / 100×100, sheet/roll sizes editable).
