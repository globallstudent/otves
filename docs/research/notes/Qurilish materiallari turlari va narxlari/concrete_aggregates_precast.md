# Binders, aggregates, ready-mix concrete and precast elements (Uzbekistan + Russia, 2025–2026): types, grades, standards, producers, prices

Research date: 2026-11-06. Price notes:
- "accessed 2026-11-06" means a live marketplace listing seen that day. Most marketplace cards (prom.uz, stroyka.uz, Russian dealer sites) carry **no price date**, so treat them as current asking prices, not verified transaction prices.
- Items marked **OLD** are dated before 2025, or are pages that are clearly stale.
- Exchange rate used for inferences: about 12,000 so'm/USD. This is derived from UzEX January 2026, where 562.7 thousand so'm ≈ $47 (source in §1).
- Russian prices from 2026 include **VAT 22%** where stated (mosbetontorg).

## 1. Cement (sement / цемент): types, old vs new marking, Uzbek & Russian producers, bag/ton prices, UzEX exchange data

### Takeaway
- **Tashkent retail:** a 50 kg bag of M400–M500 cement costs about **45,000–60,000 so'm** at mainstream Tashkent shops, with budget brands at about 29,000–37,000 so'm. Many listings price per kg instead, at about 450–750 so'm/kg, which is ≈ 22,500–37,500 so'm per 50 kg bag.
- **UzEX exchange:** about **560–600 thousand so'm/t** (Dec 2025 599.2k; Jan 2026 562.7k).
- **Moscow region:** a 50 kg bag of ЦЕМ II/А-Ш 32,5Б (ПЦ400 Д20) or ЦЕМ I 42,5Н (ПЦ500 Д0) costs about **400–535 ₽**, and bulk is about 8,200–9,300 ₽/t.
- **Other Russian regions:** a 50 kg bag is about 556–775 ₽.

### Inferences
- **Price per bag:**
  - UzEX ≈ 560–600 so'm/kg works out to ≈ 28,000–30,000 so'm per 50 kg before bagging, logistics and margin. A Tashkent retail bag of 45–60k so'm therefore carries about a 1.6–2× markup.
  - Per-kg listing prices of 450–750 so'm are consistent with traders quoting bulk or wholesale per kg.
  - Suggested catalog default for Tashkent: **M400 bag 50 kg ≈ 45,000–52,000 so'm; M500 ≈ 50,000–60,000 so'm; bulk/ton ≈ 580,000–700,000 so'm**.
- **Russia defaults:**
  - Moscow: ЦЕМ II/А-Ш 32,5Б about 400–500 ₽/bag; ЦЕМ I 42,5Н about 420–550 ₽/bag; bulk about 8,200–9,300 ₽/t.
  - Siberia and Urals bags are 10–60% higher (556–775 ₽).
- **Marking mapping for the app:**

  | GOST 31108 | Old mark |
  |---|---|
  | ЦЕМ II/А-Ш 32,5Б | ПЦ400 Д20 |
  | ЦЕМ I 32,5Н | ПЦ400 Д0 |
  | ЦЕМ II/А-Ш 42,5Н | ПЦ500 Д20 |
  | ЦЕМ I 42,5Н | ПЦ500 Д0 |

  The ЦЕМ I 32,5Н ≈ ПЦ400 Д0 row is a standard industry correspondence; only the 32,5Б and 42,5 rows are confirmed by the dealer naming above. "Б" means rapid-hardening and "Н" normal-hardening (standard GOST 31108 convention, not separately sourced here).
- "Тожик цемент HUAXIN" suggests Huaxin cement imported from Tajikistan alongside Huaxin Jizzakh output. "AKKERMANN" brand bags probably come from Akhangarancement (Akkerman-owned). These attributions are inferred from names, not confirmed.
- The low AKKERMANN 29–33k so'm prices may be for smaller bags. Verify before using.

## 2. Sand (qum / песок): types, fineness modulus, uses, Uzbek sources, prices

### Takeaway
- **Tashkent:** sand is sold mainly by the truck (6, 9, 20 m³ and larger HOWO loads), at **≈ 90,000–285,000 so'm/m³**.
  - "Chinoz qum" (Chinaz river sand) and "yuvilgan qum" (washed) are the named trade types.
  - Per-truck quotes are about 1.5–3.6 million so'm.
- **Moscow:** quarry sand is ≈ 1,100 ₽/m³; washed or river sand ≈ 1,600–1,700 ₽/m³ (VAT incl.), with a minimum of about 5 m³.

### Inferences
- **Catalog defaults, Tashkent:**
  - "Qum (Chinoz), yuvilgan / мытый" ≈ 150,000–285,000 so'm/m³.
  - "Qum, karyer (unwashed)" ≈ 90,000–150,000 so'm/m³.
  - The UI should sell by truck: КамАЗ ≈ 6–10 m³, HOWO ≈ 18–25 m³.
- **Moscow defaults:** карьерный 1,100–1,400 ₽/m³; мытый/речной 1,600–1,900 ₽/m³, plus delivery.
- **Use rules** (standard practice, consistent with GOST 8736 logic):
  - Mortar and plaster: fine to medium sand, Mk ≈ 1.5–2.5, washed.
  - Concrete: medium to coarse, Mk ≥ 2.0–2.5, class I, low clay.
  - Backfill and bedding: quarry sand.
- The Mk group boundaries commonly cited (from memory, **not verified** in this session) are:

  | Group | Mk |
  |---|---|
  | Very coarse | >3.0 |
  | Coarse | 2.5–3.0 |
  | Medium | 2.0–2.5 |
  | Fine | 1.5–2.0 |
  | Very fine | 1.0–1.5 |

### Gaps
- No dated, official Tashkent sand price (stat.uz blocked). Listing units are often unstated.
- Chinaz vs Ohangaron vs barkhan (dune) sand qualities and Mk values for Uzbek sources were not found.
- Bulk density factors (t/m³) for sand by type were not sourced. The app needs them for ton↔m³ conversion.

## 3. Crushed stone, gravel, sand-gravel mix (sheben / shag'al / klinets / kompot; щебень, гравий, ПГС)

### Takeaway
- **Tashkent:** crushed stone runs ≈ **60,000–140,000 so'm per m³** (some truck-load quotes 230,000–240,000/m³). Klinets (screenings 0–5/0–8 mm) is ≈ 150,000–180,000. Truck loads are 500k (6 m³) to 1.4M (18 m³).
