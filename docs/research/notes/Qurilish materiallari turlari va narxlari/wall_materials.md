## 1. Ceramic brick and stone (рядовой/лицевой, полнотелый/пустотелый, 1НФ/1,4НФ/2,1НФ, porous blocks, clinker): standard, properties, production, defects, producers, Russian prices

### Takeaway
In Russia, ceramic brick follows **GOST 530-2012**:
- Brick grades are M100–M300, clinker M300–M1000, and frost grades F25–F300.
- Facing brick must be at least F50, and water absorption must be at least 6% (clinker at most 6%).
- Lime inclusions and efflorescence are limited by explicit rules.

Brick is made from clay, loam or loess and fired at about **950–1050 °C**.

Moscow retail prices in 2026:
- Рядовой 1НФ M150: about **13–30 ₽/pc**, i.e. 13,000–30,000 ₽ per 1000 pcs, at 300–420 pcs per pallet.
- Facing: **15–50 ₽/pc**.
- Clinker: **33–134 ₽/pc**.
- Porous blocks of the 380 mm (10.7НФ) type: about **110–250 ₽/pc**, roughly 8,500–11,000 ₽/m³.

Yekaterinburg is cheaper: рядовой is about **16.7–27 ₽/pc**.

### Cited Findings

**Definitions (GOST 530-2012, section 3)**
- **Рядовой** (cl. 3.10) is a product that provides the masonry's operating characteristics. **Лицевой** (cl. 3.9) also serves as a decorative material.
- **Полнотелый** (cl. 3.4) is "Изделие, в котором отсутствуют пустоты или с пустотностью не более 13%". **Пустотелый** (cl. 3.5) has voids of various shapes and sizes.
- **Клинкерный** (cl. 3.8) means high strength and low water absorption, for highly aggressive environments.
- Source: [meganorm GOST 530-2012](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm/gost_gosudarstvennyj-standart/3/gost_530-2012_mezhgosudarstvennyy_standart_kirpich_i_kamen.html).

**Formats (GOST 530-2012 Tables 2–3, from the sibling notes)**
- Brick: 1НФ 250×120×65 ("одинарный"), 1,4НФ 250×120×88 ("полуторный"), 0,7НФ 250×85×65 (euro), 0,5НФ 250×60×65.
- Stone: 2,1НФ 250×120×140 ("двойной").
- Large-format stones: 10,7НФ 380×250×219 or 250×380×219, and 14,3НФ 510×250×219.
- Source: [meganorm GOST 530-2012](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm/gost_gosudarstvennyj-standart/3/gost_530-2012_mezhgosudarstvennyy_standart_kirpich_i_kamen.html), via `masonry_materials.md` §1.

**Grades (cl. 4.1.3–4.1.5)**
- Strength: brick М100–М300; clinker М300–М1000; stones М25–М300.
- Frost: F25–F300. Facing products must be at least F50, clinker at least F75.
- Density classes: 0,7 (≤700 kg/m³) through 2,4 (2010–2400 kg/m³).
- Thermal groups by dry-masonry λ (Table 6): "high efficiency" ≤0.20, then 0.20–0.24, 0.24–0.36 and 0.36–0.46; "малоэффективные (обыкновенные)" are >0.46 W/(m·°C).
- Source: same GOST 530-2012 page.

**Water absorption (cl. 5.2.4)**
- "не более 6,0% - для клинкерного кирпича"
- "не менее 6,0% - для остальных изделий"
- Source: same page.

**Lime inclusions, "дутики" (cl. 5.1.2)**
- Facing products may have single swelling (e.g. lime) inclusions no deeper than 3 mm, totalling at most 0.2% of the face area.
- Рядовые products may have swelling inclusions totalling at most 1.0% of the vertical-face area.
- The test is steaming (cl. 7.5), run once every two weeks (Table 8).
- Source: same page.

**Efflorescence, "высолы" (cl. 5.1.3)**
- "На лицевых и клинкерных изделиях не допускаются высолы". Ordinary brick is not regulated.
- Source: same page.

**Visual defects (Table 4, cl. 5.1.4–5.1.6)**
- Facing brick: chips longer than 15 mm are not allowed; up to two chips of ≤15 mm are allowed; cracks are not allowed.
- Ordinary brick: up to 4 chips longer than 15 mm and up to 4 cracks are allowed.
- **Half-bricks (половняк) may not exceed 5% of a batch.**
- A "black core" (черная сердцевина, from FeO formed during firing) and contact spots are allowed.
- Source: same page.

**Underfiring and lime inclusions in the chimney-brick standard**
- GOST 530-2012 contains no clause on недожог/пережог.
- The older chimney-brick standard GOST 8426-75 says:
  - "Недожог кирпича не допускается" (cl. 1.8). It is checked by colour and by water absorption, which may be no more than 1.5% above a properly fired reference brick (cl. 3.6).
  - "Известковые включения (дутики), вызывающие разрушение кирпича, не допускаются" (cl. 1.9).
- Source: [dnaop GOST 8426-75](https://dnaop.com/html/64036/doc-%D0%93%D0%9E%D0%A1%D0%A2_8426-75) (1975 standard, status not shown on the page).

**Raw materials (cl. 5.3)**
- Allowed: "Глинистое сырье, кремнеземистые породы (трепел, диатомит), лессы, промышленные отходы…".
- **Loess is explicitly allowed.** This is relevant for Uzbekistan's loess and loam clays.
- Source: [meganorm GOST 530-2012](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm/gost_gosudarstvennyj-standart/3/gost_530-2012_mezhgosudarstvennyy_standart_kirpich_i_kamen.html).

**Firing temperature**
- "после обжига при 950–1050 °C" the glassy phase should be no more than 8–10%.
- Drying is done in chamber or tunnel dryers, forming is done on a belt (extrusion) press, and the ring kiln dates from the mid-19th century.
- Source: [ru.wikipedia «Кирпич»](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Кирпич) [secondary].

**Production stages (teaching materials)**
- Stages: raw-material preparation → forming (plastic extrusion or semi-dry pressing) → drying → firing, often in tunnel kilns.
- Leaning (отощающие) additives reduce drying and firing shrinkage and cracking.
- Source: [elib.belstu.by](https://elib.belstu.by/bitstream/123456789/25493/1/Tehnologija%20stroitel%27noj%20keramiki_voprosy%20k%20zachetu.pdf) and [portal.sibadi.org](https://portal.sibadi.org/mod/resource/view.php?id=95622) [S].

**Porous (поризованный) blocks**
- Porosity comes from burnout additives mixed into the clay and burned out in the kiln: sawdust (the traditional choice), peat, flax shives, straw, lignin, coal waste.
- The additives also even out firing and can cut fuel use.
- Source: [journals.psu.by](https://journals.psu.by/constructions/article/view/346/290) and [elib.belstu.by](https://elib.belstu.by/bitstream/123456789/51414/1/%d0%9a%d0%b8%d0%bd%d0%b0%d1%88_%d0%98%d1%81%d0%bf%d0%be%d0%bb%d1%8c%d0%b7%d0%be%d0%b2%d0%b0%d0%bd%d0%b8%d0%b5.pdf) [S].

**Packaging (cl. 8.2–8.5)**
- Brick is shipped palletised ("в пакетированном виде"), on pallets per GOST 18343 measuring "1 x 1 м (980 x 980 мм)" or the maker's own size.
- Packs are wrapped in shrink or stretch film (cl. 5.5.2) and may be stacked at most 4 tiers high.
- Source: [meganorm GOST 530-2012](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm/gost_gosudarstvennyj-standart/3/gost_530-2012_mezhgosudarstvennyy_standart_kirpich_i_kamen.html).

### Inferences
- **Per-m³ price of porous blocks [calc]**, at ~48 pcs/m³ for 10.7НФ and no mortar:
  - Porotherm 38 ≈ 8,700 ₽/m³.
  - Braer Thermo ≈ 11,000 ₽/m³.
  - Porotherm 51 at ~35.8 pcs/m³ ≈ 9,200 ₽/m³.
- **Equivalent per 1000 pcs [calc]:** Moscow рядовой 1НФ ≈ 13,000–30,000 ₽; Yekaterinburg ≈ 16,700–27,000 ₽.
- **Defect warnings for the app.** The quoted limits come from the findings above; the checks marked ✱ are practical rules of thumb with no source in these notes.
  - **недожог** (underfired): GOST 8426-75 detects it by a paler colour than a reference brick and by higher water absorption. ✱ In practice it also gives a dull sound when struck, crumbles, and has low frost resistance. Show a warning: "не для цоколя/фасада".
  - **пережог** (overfired): ✱ dark, glassy, often warped. It is strong but makes poor masonry (bad mortar bond and geometry).
  - **дутики / известковые включения**: lime grains that slake and swell after wetting and pop chips off. GOST 530 limits them to 0.2% (facing) or 1.0% (ordinary) of face area.
  - **высолы**: white efflorescence. GOST 530 forbids it on facing and clinker brick. ✱ In practice it also comes from salts in the mortar or water.
- **Catalog entry draft: "Кирпич керамический рядовой полнотелый 1НФ" / UZ: "Pishgan g'isht (oddiy, to'liq)"**
  - Standard: GOST 530-2012. Size: 250×120×65.
  - Grades: M100–M300 (default M150), F25–F100 (default F35/F50).
  - Density: ~1,600–2,000 kg/m³. Water absorption ≥6%. λ group: "обыкновенные" >0.46 W/(m·°C).
  - Weight: 3.3–3.7 kg. Pallet (RU): 300–480 pcs.
- **Catalog entry draft: "…пустотелый 1,4НФ"**
  - Size: 250×120×88. Same grade ranges.
  - Pallet (RU): ~288–416 pcs, manufacturer-specific.
- **Catalog entry draft: "Камень керамический поризованный 10,7НФ / 14,3НФ"**
  - Sizes: 380×250×219 or 510×250×219.
  - Grades: M75–M150 (M100 typical), F50. λ ≈ 0.14–0.15 W/(m·°C) (maker figure).
  - Weight: 14.7–19 kg. 50–60 pcs/pallet.

## 2. Uzbekistan fired brick («pishgan g'isht», «жжёный кирпич»): sizes, grades actually sold, plants, clay, kilns, prices in Tashkent

### Takeaway
In Tashkent in November 2026, ordinary fired brick sells **by the piece** at about **550–1,250 so'm**. Most OLX listings sit at 750–900 so'm, which is ≈550,000–1,250,000 so'm per 1000 pcs, or about $46–104.

A VAT-invoiced plant in Akhangaran asks noticeably more:
- Ordinary brick: **1,950 so'm**.
- Facing brick: **2,400 so'm**.

**Grades and formats.** Grades advertised are mostly **M75–M170**, with M125–M150 the most common.

Local formats are often non-GOST: 25×12×6.5, "полтора/poltara" 25×12×8.8–9, plus old 28×12×6 and 28×12×8 cards. Sellers count in **trucks of ~7,200 pcs** or **packs of ~500 pcs**.

**Production.** Most plants are in Tashkent region (Akhangaran/Angren cluster, Kibray, Yangiyul), Samarkand and Fergana valley. Since the 2019–2020 gas cut-off they run on **coal**.

Two kiln types appear:
- Older **ring (Hoffmann-type) kilns** (кольцевая печь), still in use.
- Newer Chinese/European lines, some with **tunnel kilns**.

## 3. Silicate (sand-lime) brick, GOST 379-2015: production, properties, Russian prices, seismic prohibition in Uzbekistan

### Takeaway
Silicate brick is pressed from quartz sand (~92–94%) and lime (~6–8%) and hardened in an autoclave at about 175–200 °C and 0.9–1.6 MPa.

It is covered by **GOST 379-2015**:
- Grades M100–M300 and F25–F100; water absorption at least 6%.
- Uzbekistan (Uzstandart) voted for the standard.
- A secondary source says GOST 379-2025 replaced it from 1 May 2026; this is unverified.

**Uzbekistan's seismic code KMK 2.01.03-19 cl. 3.5.3 bans silicate brick in seismic regions**, which covers practically all of the country. The app should block it for Uzbek projects. Russia's SP 14.13330 has no explicit ban.

Prices:
- Moscow: 1НФ **17–28.5 ₽/pc**.
- Yekaterinburg: **12–19 ₽**.
- Pallets hold more pieces than ceramic brick: МЗСК ships 616–668 pcs/pallet.

### Cited Findings
- **Definition (GOST 379-2015 §1).** Silicate products are made by "прессования увлажненной смеси из кремнеземистых материалов и извести" and hardened by "гидротермальной обработки в автоклаве". Source: [meganorm GOST 379-2015](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm/gost_gosudarstvennyj-standart/2/gost_379-2015_mezhgosudarstvennyy_standart_kirpich_kamni.html) [C].
- **Sizes and tolerances.**
  - Single brick 250×120×65 (cl. 3.1); thickened brick 250×120×88 (cl. 3.2); stone 250×120×**138** (cl. 3.3).
  - A block is a product with header width >130 mm (cl. 3.4).
  - Tolerance is ±2 mm, or ±1 mm for thin-joint/glue masonry (cl. 4.2.2).
  - Source: same page.
- **Grades and properties.**
  - Strength M100–M300 (cl. 4.1.4); frost F25–F100 (cl. 4.1.5).
  - Density classes 1,0–2,2 (Table 1).
  - "Водопоглощение изделий должно быть не менее 6%" (cl. 5.1.3.4).
  - Facing brick must be at least M125 (cl. 5.1.3.1).
  - Hollow-unit outer walls must be at least 10 mm thick (cl. 4.1.3).
  - Source: same page.
- **Defects.**
  - Ordinary products: no more than three inclusions of clay, sand or lime over 5 mm.
  - Facing products: no such inclusions on the face.
  - Not allowed: "сетки мелких трещин от непогасившейся силикатной массы" (cl. 5.1.1.8).
  - Half-bricks: ≤5% for ordinary and ≤2% for facing (cl. 5.1.2).
  - Source: same page.
- **Restrictions on use.** The text of GOST 379-2015 contains **no** explicit ban on use in foundations, plinths, wet rooms, stoves or seismic zones. Source: same page.
- **Uzbekistan voted.** The list is AZ, AM, BY, KZ, KG, RU and **UZ (Узстандарт)**. Source: same page.
- **Mix composition** (a university lecture): quartz sand 92–94, air lime 6–8, water 7–8 (parts by mass). Source: [bsut.by lecture](https://www.bsut.by/images/MainMenuFiles/Obrazovanie/Kafedry/Arhitektura/lekcii/silicats.pdf) [S].
- **Autoclave conditions.**
  - Autoclaved materials are treated in saturated steam at **175–200 °C and 0.9–1.6 MPa**. Source: [Большая российская энциклопедия «Автоклавные материалы»](https://bigenc.ru/c/avtoklavnye-materialy-90538c) [S].
  - One maker states 10–14 h hardening at 8–12 atm. Source: [bck.by](https://bck.by/product/kirpich/kirpich-silikatnyy) [S].
- **Seismic ban in Uzbekistan.** KMK 2.01.03-19 cl. 3.5.3: "Применение силикатного кирпича в сейсмических районах запрещается". The same clause also bans fired brick and stone with horizontal voids. Source: [KMK 2.01.03-19 (eis.su)](https://eis.su/srostandart/kmk-2-01-03-19/), via `seismic_uzbek_norms.md` §3.3.
- **No explicit ban in Russia.** Expert commentary says SP 14.13330.2018 does not explicitly prohibit silicate brick. Source: [normacs.info](https://www.normacs.info/answers/6249).
- **Russian prices.**
  - МЗСК (Михайловский), price list valid from **08.04.2026** [C]:
    - White рядовой 1,4НФ: **22.00 ₽**, 668 pcs/pallet, 14,696 ₽/pallet.
    - White лицевой полнотелый 1НФ: 28.50 ₽, 616 pcs/pallet, 17,556 ₽/pallet.
    - The same article says **GOST 379-2025 replaced GOST 379-2015 from 1 May 2026** (not independently verified).
    - Source: [gostcheck.ru](https://gostcheck.ru/articles/ceny-na-kirpich-2026).
  - Moscow, Kirpich.ru [C]:
    - Борский 1НФ полнотелый 17 ₽; 1,4НФ 18.4 ₽.
    - ТЛК 1НФ М150 20.4 ₽; 1,4НФ 21.5 ₽.
    - Source: [kirpich.ru силикатный](https://www.kirpich.ru/shop/kirpich/kirpich_silikatnyy/).
  - Yekaterinburg, brick-66 [C]:

### Inferences

## 4. Autoclaved aerated concrete (газобетон, «gazoblok»), GOST 31359-2024 / 31360-2024, and non-autoclaved foam block (пеноблок), GOST 25485-2019

### Takeaway
Autoclaved aerated concrete (AAC) is made from cement and/or lime, finely ground quartz sand or fly ash, aluminium powder or paste as the gas former, gypsum and water. It is cut into blocks and autoclaved.

**Standards:**
- **GOST 31359-2024** covers the material; Uzbekistan voted for it.
- **GOST 31360-2024** covers the blocks; it has been in force in Russia since 01.01.2025.

**Common grades:**
- Wall blocks: D400–D600, B2–B5.
- Load-bearing walls in seismic areas need at least B3.5 and D600 under SP 14.13330. KMK 2.01.03-19 allows cellular blocks below B3.5 only under separate norms.

**Foam block** (non-autoclaved, GOST 25485-2019, which Uzbekistan also voted for) may shrink up to **3 mm/m**, against **0.5 mm/m** for AAC made on quartz sand. This is the main quality warning.

**Prices:**
- Moscow D500: **6,500–8,200 ₽/m³** (Bonolit 7,400 ₽/m³, Ytong ~6,800–8,085).
- Yekaterinburg: **4,300–5,900 ₽/m³**.
- Foam block D600: **~5,000–6,500 ₽/m³**.
- AAC prices fell about 30% in 2024–2025.

**Uzbekistan:**
- Large Chinese-equipped autoclaved plants opened in 2022–2025:
  - ALIGAZABLOK in Akhangaran, 500k m³/yr.
  - Megaton in Namangan FEZ, 600k m³/yr.
  - Arton in Urgench, 200k m³/yr.
  - Ecoton Sharq in Angren, 300k m³/yr.
- Tashkent AAC prices: **440,000–850,000 so'm/m³**, about $37–71; most offers are 550,000–700,000.
- A 600×300×200 block sells for about **19,000 so'm**.
- Penoblok (foam block): about **5,000–10,000 so'm/pc**.

### Cited Findings

**GOST 31359-2024 (autoclaved cellular concrete)**
- Effective in Russia from 01.01.2025 (Rosstandart order 532-ст of 24.04.2024); replaces GOST 31359-2007.
- Voting countries include **Uzbekistan (UZ, Узстандарт)**, together with AM, BY, KZ, KG, RU and TJ.
- Source: [GOST 31359-2024 PDF (istkult.ru)](https://istkult.ru/upload/medialibrary/fcd/bp5noycb0cvz9sm0psf01d6ft0wt11ww/GOST-31359_2024.-Mezhgosudarstvennyy-standart.-Betony-yacheistye-avtoklavnogo-tverdeniya.-Tekhnicheskie-usloviya.pdf) [C, text extracted from the PDF].
- **Definition (cl. 3.1):** cellular concrete made from "вяжущего, тонкодисперсного кремнеземистого компонента, порообразователя и воды", given heat-moisture treatment at elevated pressure.
- **Raw materials (cl. 4.13):**
  - Binders: Portland cement (GOST 30515/31108), lime (GOST 9179), and high-basic ash with active CaO ≥40%.
  - Siliceous component: sand (GOST 8736), TPP fly ash, ore-processing waste, or recycled own cut-offs.
  - "В качестве газообразователя рекомендуется применять алюминиевую пудру по ГОСТ 5494 или пасту".
  - Additives: blast-furnace slag, gypsum.
- **Grades (cl. 4.5–4.7):**
  - Strength classes B0,35–B10.
  - Density grades **D100–D700 and D800**, in steps of 50.
  - Frost grades F15, F25, F35, F50, F75.
- **Strength vs density (Table Б.1).** These are the strength classes the standard allows for each density grade:

| Density grade | Allowed strength classes |
|---|---|
| D300 | B1.5; B2 |
| D400 | B1.5 to B3.5 |
| D500 | B2 to B4.5 |
| D600 | B2.5 to B6 |
| D700 | B3.5 to B10 |

  - Table Б.1 also labels the concrete types: **конструкционно-теплоизоляционный** is D300–D700 with B1.5 or higher; **конструкционный** is D800 with B7.5–B10.
- **Drying shrinkage (cl. 4.9):** ≤**0.5 mm/m** on quartz sand; ≤0.7 mm/m on other siliceous components; ≤1.0 mm/m for insulating grades.
- **Other properties:** non-combustible, class НГ (cl. 4.12); radionuclides Aэфф ≤370 Bq/kg (cl. 4.11).
- **No λ table.** GOST 31359-2024 gives no thermal conductivity table; λ must come from product standards or specifications (cl. 4.10).

**GOST 31360-2024 (blocks), from the sibling notes**
- Effective in Russia **01.01.2025**.
- **Accuracy categories:**
  - Category I: ±3 / ±2 / ±1 mm, intended for thin-joint glue.
  - Category II: ±4 / ±3 / ±4 mm, laid on mortar.
- **Minimum classes:** strength at least B1.5. Frost grade F25 for external walls, F15 for other products.
- **Maximum unit size:** 1500×650×1000 mm.
- **Designation example:** `Блок I/600×200×300/D500/B2,5/F25 ГОСТ 31360-2024`.
- Source: [meganorm GOST 31360-2024](https://meganorm.ru/mega_doc/norm/gost_gosudarstvennyj-standart/0/gost_31360-2024_mezhgosudarstvennyy_standart_izdeliya_dlya.html), via `masonry_materials.md` §2.

**Thermal conductivity**
- From a comparison in «Энергосбережение» №3-2016 (Горшков, Ватин, Корниенко, Пестряков):
  - **Dry λ0:** D400 = 0.096 (GOST 31359-2007) vs 0.110 (SP 50.13330); D600 = 0.140 vs 0.140 W/(m·K).
  - **Design moisture:** 4% (A) and 5% (Б) per GOST 31359-2007, vs **8% and 12%** per SP 50.13330.
  - **Design λ:** SP 50.13330 gives λA = 0.140 and λБ = 0.150 for D400, and λA = 0.220 and λБ = 0.260 for D600.
  - Source: [ABOK, «Соответствие стен из автоклавного газобетона…», 2016](https://www.abok.ru/for_spec/articles/31/6387/6387.pdf) [C, text extracted from the PDF].
- A Wikipedia table gives λ dry / at 5% moisture:

| Grade | Dry | At 5% moisture |
|---|---|---|
| D300 | 0.072 | 0.088 |
| D400 | 0.096 | 0.117 |
| D500 | 0.112 | 0.147 |
| D600 | 0.141 | 0.183 |

  - The article's text gives D500 dry as 0.12, which conflicts with its own table, and the article is flagged for lacking sources.
  - Source: [ru.wikipedia «Газобетон»](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Газобетон) [secondary].
- A maker's price list (01.11.2024, **OLD**) gives D500 B2.5–B3.5 λ 0.112, F100. Source: [VK-block price list](https://the-brick.ru/zadmin_data/file.attach/429653/VK-block_01.11.2024.pdf) [S].

**Production process**
- Mix → aluminium reacts with the alkaline slurry, releases hydrogen and the mass rises → pre-cure → cut into blocks → autoclave in saturated steam. The non-autoclaved variant is cured naturally or by electric heating.
- Source: [ru.wikipedia «Газобетон»](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Газобетон) [secondary].

  - An OLX search for "guvala" returned **0 listings** on 2026-11-06. Source: [OLX q-guvala](https://www.olx.uz/dom-i-sad/q-guvala/) [C].
  - Unfired raw brick ("xom g'isht", кирпич-сырец) is sold on prom.uz at **440–950 so'm/pc** [C]. Many sellers list "pishgan + xom" together at one price, so the raw-brick price is uncertain. Source: [prom.uz Кирпичи](https://www.prom.uz/section/kirpichi/).
- **Chimney brick.** The clay chimney-brick standard GOST 8426-75 covers brick for heating up to 700 °C (250×120×65 / 88, Мрз 25–50). Source: [dnaop GOST 8426-75](https://dnaop.com/html/64036/doc-%D0%93%D0%9E%D0%A1%D0%A2_8426-75).
- **Adobe in KMK 2.01.03-19:**
  - Low-strength materials (<3.5 MPa: adobe/сырцовый кирпич, earth blocks, грунтобетон) are allowed for 1 storey (4.5 m) at 7 points only, with a stone plinth.
  - With a timber frame: 2 storeys at 7 points, 1 at 8, not allowed at 9.
  - Earth-fill roofs are banned.
  - Source: [KMK 2.01.03-19](https://eis.su/srostandart/kmk-2-01-03-19/), via `seismic_uzbek_norms.md` §3.14.
- **Clay housing and earthquake losses.** More than 65% of rural housing in Central Asia is built from clay materials, and most earthquake casualties and damage in the last 60 years were in individual houses. Source: [cyberleninka, Ismailov & Khadzhiev](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/effective-methods-improving-the-load-bearing-ability-of-clay-bricks-and-walls).
- **Traditional techniques.** Traditional Bukhara construction used monolithic and moulded paxsa (rammed or cob earth), sun-dried and fired brick. Source: [inscience.uz](https://inscience.uz/index.php/socinov/article/view/6900).
- **Sun-dried brick.** It is made from unfired clay, historically with chopped straw. Source: [ru.wikipedia «Кирпич»](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Кирпич).

### Inferences
- **Fireplace and stove rule for the app.** Line the firebox with ШБ/ША fireclay brick on fireclay mortar, not ordinary cement mortar. ✱ Use ceramic solid brick M150 or higher for the stove body and chimney; never silicate brick or hollow brick in contact with flue gases. The ban on hollow brick and silicate brick is a rule of thumb, not sourced here (see the sibling `architecture_fireplace_pool_rules.md`).
- **Catalog entry draft: "Кирпич шамотный ШБ-5" / UZ: "Shamot g'isht (o'tga chidamli g'isht) ШБ-5"**
  - Standard: GOST 390-2018.
  - Size: 230×114×65. Weight ~3.5 kg.
  - Service temperature ≤1350 °C (ША ≤1400 °C).
  - Pallet ~350 pcs (Russia).
  - Price: RU 99–120 ₽; UZ 11,000–15,000 so'm.
- **Catalog entry draft: "Кирпич облицовочный (лицевой)" / UZ: "Pardozbop (oblitsovka) g'isht"**
  - Standard: GOST 530-2012.
  - Sizes: 1НФ 250×120×65 or 0,7НФ 250×85×65.
  - Grades: M150 or higher; F50 or higher by the standard (Uzbek listings show F25).
  - Water absorption 6–14% (clinker ≤6%). No efflorescence allowed.
  - Price: RU 15–50 ₽; UZ 2,000–6,000 so'm local, 12,000–17,000 imported.
- **Adobe in the app:**
  - Treat "Guvala / paxsa / xom g'isht" as a "legacy / not recommended" material that triggers the KMK §3.7 limits.
  - Show a price only for xom g'isht, at ~440–950 so'm, marked as uncertain.

### Gaps

## 7. Packaging, selling units and delivery practice

### Takeaway
**Russia.** Plants sell brick **by the piece, priced by the pallet**:
- Рядовой 1НФ: **300–480 pcs per pallet**.
- Silicate: 600–670 pcs.
- Porous 10.7НФ blocks: 50–60 pcs.
- AAC: sold **per m³**, 1.5–1.8 m³ per pallet.

Pallets and film are charged separately. Delivery is by длинномер (flatbed, up to ~8,000 bricks) or манипулятор (crane truck, ~3,840 bricks, unloading included). In Moscow region a delivery trip costs about **19,000–45,000 ₽** depending on distance.

**Uzbekistan.** Sellers quote brick **per piece (dona)**:
- Lots are counted in **trucks of ~7,200 pcs** or **packs of ~500 pcs**.
- Gazoblok is sold per m³ or per piece; one producer ships 48 blocks (1.728 m³) per pallet.
- No published delivery tariffs or pallet deposits were found.

### Inferences
- **Data model.** Store these fields per item:
  - `sell_unit`: piece | m³ | pallet | 1000 pcs.
  - `pcs_per_pallet`, `m3_per_pallet`, `weight_kg`.
  - `pallet_deposit`: editable, empty by default.
  - `delivery_options`: list of options (длинномер, манипулятор, KamAZ), each with a capacity in pieces and a per-trip price by distance band.
- **Rounding.** Round brick quantities up to whole pallets when the supplier requires it (Голицыно does), and to trucks in Uzbekistan when the user picks a truck lot.
- **Uzbek implied delivery cost [calc].** In the M170 listing the small-lot price with delivery is 1,250 so'm/pc, against 1,130 for 1–5 trucks.
