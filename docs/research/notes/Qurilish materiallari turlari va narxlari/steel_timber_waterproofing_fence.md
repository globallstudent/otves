## 1. Rebar (арматура / armatura), binding wire (вязальная проволока), masonry mesh (сетка кладочная Вр-1)

### Takeaway
In Tashkent, Uzbek (Bekabad / Uzmetkombinat) A500C/A400 rebar is listed at **~9.0 mln so'm/t (~$750/t) as of 04.11.2026**. Russian and Kazakh imports are usually a little cheaper at **~8.0–8.9 mln so'm/t**, and the overall market range is 8–9.6 mln so'm/t. In Moscow, A500C Ø12 has a **median price of ~52,000 ₽/t** (range 40–71k), which matches the Cbonds Russia A500C index of **52,050 ₽/t on 06.11.2026**. Binding wire in Tashkent costs ~10.3–11.4k so'm/kg. Masonry mesh 50×50×4 Вр-1 in Russia costs ~278–292 ₽/m².

### Cited Findings

**Standard (ГОСТ 34028-2016)**
- ГОСТ 34028-2016 «Прокат арматурный для железобетонных конструкций. ТУ» covers smooth (гладкий) and ribbed (периодический) rebar of classes A240, A400, A500, A600 (plus Ап600, A800, A1000). A240 comes only as smooth bar. A400 and higher are ribbed — [docs.cntd.ru](http://docs.cntd.ru/document/1200144936); [meganorm.ru](https://meganorm.ru/Data2/1/4293746/4293746568.htm); summarized in [search result on GOST text](https://files.stroyinf.ru/Data2/1/4293746/4293746568.pdf)
- Minimum yield strength is 240 (A240), 390 (A400) and 500 (A500) N/mm². In designations, "С" = weldable (свариваемая) and "Н" = higher ductility, e.g. «А500СН» — [GOST text, files.stroyinf.ru](https://files.stroyinf.ru/Data2/1/4293746/4293746568.pdf)
- Uzbek listings still use old ГОСТ 5781-82 names and steel grades: «А3 / АIII», «А-1 (А240)», «35ГС», «25Г2С», «Ст3сп/пс». Bars are commonly 11.7 m long (also 6 m, and coils/бухты for Ø6–10) — [prom.uz armatura section](https://www.prom.uz/section/armatura/)

### Inferences
- Tashkent benchmark for the app (Nov 2026):
  - Uzbek A400/A500C: **8.6 (exchange EXW) – 9.3 mln so'm/t (~$720–780)**
  - Russian/Kazakh A500C: **8.0–8.9 mln so'm/t**
  - A240 smooth Ø6–10: **~6.0 mln so'm/t** (fewer data points)
- In Russia the typical ex-warehouse price is **50–56k ₽/t (≈ $610–690; ≈ 7.3–8.2 mln so'm)**. The Tashkent price of Russian rebar (8.15–8.2 mln) is only ~5–10% above the Moscow level.
- The app should store rebar priced per tonne but let the user enter metres. Standard nominal masses: Ø6 0.222, Ø8 0.395, Ø10 0.617, Ø12 0.888, Ø14 1.21, Ø16 1.58, Ø18 2.00, Ø20 2.47, Ø22 2.98, Ø25 3.85 kg/m. These are standard reference values, not sourced in this session — verify against the ГОСТ 34028-2016 tables.
- Typical private-house use:
  - Strip foundation: A500C Ø10–14 longitudinal, Ø6–8 A240 stirrups
  - Slab: Ø10–12
  - Belts/lintels: Ø10–12
  
  This is engineering convention, not sourced here.

### Inferences
- The typical Uzbek plot wall ("devor") is a strip footing plus brick/block wall or pillars every ~3 m with infill. No source priced it per metre in so'm. The app should build it bottom-up from components:
  - Footing concrete + rebar (§1)
  - Bricks/blocks (other researcher)
  - Profnastil/3D/rabitsa infill per m²
  - Profile-pipe posts/lags per m (§2)
  - Labour
- A Moscow-style metal-post profnastil fence 2 m high uses ≈2 m² sheet per metre. In Tashkent this costs ~95–135k so'm/m for polymer sheet at 46.5–68k so'm/m², plus posts 60×60 and two lags 40×20 per metre. This is a derived estimate, not a sourced benchmark.

### Gaps
- No credible so'm turnkey per-metre benchmark was found for brick devor, profnastil or 3D fences in Tashkent. No 2026 Tashkent price was found for rabitsa or gates (откатные/распашные ворота).
- No price was found for 3D panels in Russia, or for евроштакетник.
